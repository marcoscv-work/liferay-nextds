Typography

Base typeface is Rawline (sans-serif), with sensible web-safe fallbacks.

Display · 3rem Build accessible products

Title XL · 2.25rem Find a public service

Title LG · 1.75rem Section heading

Title MD · 1.375rem Subsection heading

Lead · 1.25rem A lead paragraph introduces the topic.

Body · 1rem Body copy is used for most page content.

Body SM · 0.875rem Small print and helper text.
